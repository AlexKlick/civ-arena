## How it's implemented

- Dispatch: `build_runtime` returns a `ScriptedRuntime` for "turtler" (src/civ_arena/agents/runtime.py:94-95); `take_turn` just calls `run_policy` (runtime.py:41-44). Per-agent `random.Random(profile.seed)` (runtime.py:37-39; seed 2202 in configs/llm-vs-turtler-005.yaml:32).
- Turn flow (`run_policy` scripted.py:53-122): reads overview/units/cities (:59-62); visible map only on turn%7==1 (:63-64); research = first doctrine tech present in `available_research` (:67-71) — the currently-researching tech is still listed (rules.py:120-129 never excludes it), so most turns re-issue `set_research` and eat an `ALREADY` rejection (rules.py:360-361); every city re-sets production every turn (:79-83; replaces the queue with a single item, rules.py:504-511); purchase only when turn%3==0 and gold ≥ 120 (:84-91) at 2× production cost (rules.py:149-154) → MONUMENT 100, GRANARY 120, WARRIOR 80 gold.
- `_pick_build` (scripted.py:164-173): below 2 cities wanted = [SETTLER, *build_order]; at/above cap → WALLS→MONUMENT→WARRIOR→SPEARMAN, first item the city can legally produce. WALLS (70 prod, state.py:59) has its `defhp` field read nowhere in the sim — `attack` only ever targets units (rules.py:299-318), there is no city-capture mechanic in rules.py — so the top build priority is mechanically inert.
- Settlers (`_handle_settlers` scripted.py:141-154): found when >2 hexes from any city in the projected view (:146; mirrors legality rules.py:343-345), else walk toward hardcoded (2,2)/(−2,−2) (:153). The 2nd opening settler (roster 2×SETTLER+2×WARRIOR+1×SCOUT, layouts.py:111) founds city #2 by ~turn 2-3; the cap then forbids settlers forever.
- Military: attack rule is doctrine-independent — nearest foreign unit in melee-1/archer-2 range (:125-138, rng_max at :130), capped at 2 attacks/turn (:97, :103). It is not pacifist; it just doesn't seek. No target + `fortify_idle` → fortify once (:106-107); FORT_BONUS +4 defense (state.py:76, applied rules.py:448).
- Fortified units keep movement == max, so damaged units heal 10/turn at begin_phase (engine.py:51-57) — the heal rule expansionist forfeits.
- Completeness gate: idle fortified units pass `end_turn` first try (referee.py:314-322 lists only movement>0-and-unfortified), but the opening scout (mv 3, one 1-cost step per turn via `_march`, scripted.py:157-161) leaves movement on the table almost every turn → one structured `unmoved_units` bounce per (player,turn) (referee.py:230-243), answered by fortify + re-end (scripted.py:117-121) while the scout lives.
- RNG discipline: seeded per-agent rng used only for purchase idempotency keys (:90) and the scout's random-corner pick (:110-112, `_CORNERS` :38); checkpointed in `rng_states` (arena/checkpoints.py:24) via getstate round-trip (canonical.py:130-140), restored by `load_rng` (runtime.py:124-127). Combat draws use the sim's separate rng (seed*7919+13, layouts.py:92; rules.py:450-458).
- Turn action budget: no tool-round cap for scripted seats (only LLM seats get `max_tool_rounds`); effective budget = per-unit movement (mv 2, scout 3; state.py:33-37), one move per unit per turn (`_march` returns after one accepted step), ≤2 attacks total (attack zeroes movement, rules.py:439-441), purchases on the turn%3/gold≥120 gate, map read on turn%7==1, plus the one-bounce gate. Steady state ≈ 8-12 facade calls/turn.

## What it optimizes
- Nominally techs (30 each) and population (20 each) on a fixed 2-city base, plus units (10 each) from WARRIOR/SPEARMAN and gold trickle (MONUMENT +2, GRANARY +1 per turn; engine.py:101-103). Weights at value.py:18-24; components counted by score_vector (value.py:27-37).
- The build order does NOT serve that: WALLS-first spends ~70 prod (≈14 turns at 5/turn for a pop-1 city, engine.py:30-33) per city on a building worth zero score components and zero mechanics. MONUMENT/GRANARY — the only economy items, GRANARY raising the pop cap 7→9 (engine.py:26-27, :36-37) — sit behind it.
- Research order is also score-neutral: no tech modifies any yield in engine.py (production/food/science/gold never consult TECHS), and both doctrines' lists total 245 (state.py:62-71), so the tech count converges; ordering only shifts unit-unlock timing — turtler gets SPEARMAN (str 25) third via BRONZE_WORKING while expansionist gets ARCHER second (UNIT_TECH_REQ, state.py:54).
- Under the differential objective (own score minus strongest rival, value.py:62-71) it is a bounded strategy: ceiling fixed by the 2-city cap; it wins by attrition when rivals fail, not by compounding.

## Expected failure modes
- Structural score deficit: each rival city is +100 plus compounding pop/science (science = 2×pop per city, engine.py:94); a 3-4 city opponent is mathematically out of reach within max_turns=40 (configs/llm-vs-turtler-005.yaml:7).
- WALLS is pure queue latency (defhp unread; no city combat exists anywhere in rules.py) — its first ~14 turns of production per city buy nothing.
- Same cap-corruption bug as expansionist: `_pick_build` counts the projected city list (scripted.py:81), which includes currently-observed foreign cities (visibility.py:178-200) — a rival city sighted before settler #2 founds turns the settler branch off, leaving a 0-strength settler walking to (−2,−2) forever (scripted.py:153).
- No power projection: fortified spearmen defend well (+4, rules.py:448; melee counterattack rules.py:452-459) but cities can't be captured, so defense protects nothing the score measures; an ARCHER parked at distance 2 farms its perimeter with no counter (ranged branch rules.py:449-451).
- Purchase pref starves growth: MONUMENT (100) before GRANARY (120) delays the pop-cap raise; once both exist it defaults to WARRIOR (80) padding the cheapest component (units, 10).
- Brittle ordering generally: no conditionality on game state — the queue never reacts to threats, gold, or rival tech, so parameter tweaks (build_order permutations) are the entire strategy space.

- AMBIENT rows: unit.refreshed, unit.healed (10/turn) for damaged parked units (engine.py:56), city.food/city.produced, city.building_built (WALLS then MONUMENT), player.income stepping +2 after each MONUMENT, player.tech_researched arriving in MINING→MASONRY→BRONZE_WORKING order (engine.py:152-167).
- Score components over time (summary shape, coordinator.py:418-434): cities flat at 2 from ~turn 3 (city names `<agent>-t<turn>`, scripted.py:149); population slow S-curve toward the 7 (or 9 with GRANARY) per-city cap; techs steady (science = 4/turn at 2×pop-1, accelerating with growth; full 8 techs rarely finished by turn 40); units accumulate WARRIOR/SPEARMAN with few losses (fortified + healing); gold climbs to 120 then drops 100/120/80 every gated turn, net drift slightly positive from building income.
- Distinguishing fingerprint vs expansionist: cities curve flat at 2, unit.healed rows present, no military move_unit after turn ~3, WALLS in the production_set history.

## Variance sensitivity
- Seat position: low-medium. Behavior is position-independent by construction (no march, home fortify), so per-seat trajectories are near-clones; only sequential phase order (simulator.py:91-96) and which rival arrives first differ. Hardcoded binary coords remain: settler fallback (−2,−2) for every pid≠0 (scripted.py:153) and scout `_CORNERS` fixed to radius-5 edge points (:38) that become mid-map on radius-7.
- Map seed: low. Starts and ring-1 are sanitized passable land (layouts.py:73-77) and the doctrine never depends on distant terrain; the main seed noise is combat rolls (rules.py:87-90) and its ring-2 food basket pacing growth (engine.py:70-91).
- Opponent count (2→4): hurts. The objective is own-minus-max-rival (value.py:62-71), so each additional seat raises the chance someone runs wide expansion unopposed while turtler's fixed ceiling cannot respond; more neighbors also raise the odds an early observed foreign city corrupts its 2-city settler logic, and more scouts/armies crossing its borders mean more opportunistic 10-pt unit trades it cannot replace quickly at 5 prod/turn. It is the steadiest, lowest-variance seat — a natural baseline for the glm-flash analyst: `aggression > 0` converts its fortified stack into area denial, and a modest `expand_ring` softens the 2-city cap without full settler spam.
