## How it's implemented
- Doctrine record: `DOCTRINES["expansionist"]` at src/civ_arena/agents/scripted.py:17-25 — research POTTERY→ARCHERY→WRITING→MINING→MASONRY→BRONZE_WORKING→ANIMAL_HUSBANDRY→IRRIGATION, `march: True`, `fortify_idle: False`, build_order SETTLER,WARRIOR,ARCHER,SCOUT, purchase_pref SCOUT,WARRIOR,MONUMENT,GRANARY, `max_cities: 4`.
- Dispatch: `build_runtime` → `ScriptedRuntime` (runtime.py:92-93); `take_turn` calls `run_policy` (runtime.py:41-44); per-agent `random.Random(profile.seed)` (runtime.py:37-39).
- Turn flow (`run_policy` scripted.py:53-122): overview/units/cities (:59-62), visible map on turn%7==1 (:63-64), research = first doctrine tech in `available_research` (:67-71; the researching tech stays listed, rules.py:120-129, so most set_research calls bounce `ALREADY`, rules.py:360-361), per-city production re-set every turn (:79-83; queue replaced by one item, rules.py:504-511), purchase when turn%3==0 and gold ≥ 120 (:84-91) at 2× cost (rules.py:149-154).
- `_pick_build` (scripted.py:164-168): while `len(cities) < max_cities` the wanted list is [SETTLER, *build_order] — SETTLER has no tech gate (rules.py:132-141), so every city below the cap builds 80-prod settlers back-to-back (state.py:33); at ≥4 it becomes WARRIOR→ARCHER→SCOUT. Caveat: `cities` is the projected list — own plus currently-observed foreign (visibility.py:178-200) — so enemy cities in sight count toward the cap.
- Settlers (`_handle_settlers` scripted.py:141-154): found when >2 hexes from any city in view (:146), else walk toward hardcoded (2,2)/(−2,−2) (:153); founding consumes the settler, creates a pop-1 city with border_radius 2 and claims its tiles (rules.py:469-494).
- Military: identical opportunistic attack rule as turtler — nearest foreign in melee-1/archer-2 (:125-138), ≤2 attacks/turn (:97, :103), attack zeroes the attacker's movement (rules.py:439-441). No target + `fortify_idle: False` + `march: True` → every idle military unit walks one step per turn toward hardcoded (4,−3) if pid==0 else (−4,3) (:108-109). Scouts (mv 3) pick a random `_CORNERS` target each turn (:110-112, :38).
- Movement discipline: `_march` tries up to 3 distance-sorted neighbor steps and returns after ONE accepted move (:45-50, :157-161) — mv-2 units spend 1 of 2 movement. Leftover movement trips the referee's one-per-(player,turn) completeness bounce (referee.py:230-243; unmoved = movement>0 and not fortified, :314-322), which `run_policy` answers with fortify + re-end (scripted.py:117-121). Marchers therefore end turns fortified but never heal (heal needs movement==max at begin_phase, engine.py:51-57).
- RNG discipline: per-agent seeded rng (runtime.py:37-39) drawn for purchase keys (:90) and scout corner picks (:111); checkpointed via `rng_states` (checkpoints.py:24) with getstate serialization (canonical.py:130-140). Combat uses the sim's separate rng (layouts.py:92; rules.py:450-458), so identical seeds replay identically.
- Turn action budget: no tool-round cap for scripted seats; effective budget = per-unit movement (mv 2, scout 3; state.py:33-37), one move per unit per turn, ≤2 attacks total, purchases on the turn%3/gold≥120 gate, map read on turn%7==1, one completeness bounce. Roughly 10-20 facade calls/turn depending on unit count.

## What it optimizes
- Chases cities (weight 100, value.py:18-24) almost exclusively: settler-first-until-4 converts production into +100 per city plus each new city's compounding outputs (production 3+2×pop, science 2×pop, engine.py:30-33, :94) and population points (20 each, value.py:33).
- The research list does not serve that beyond +30/tech: nothing in engine.py reads TECHS for yields, and both lists total 245 (state.py:62-71), so the reachable tech count is identical — ordering only shifts the ARCHER unlock (ARCHERY 2nd) vs turtler's SPEARMAN (BRONZE_WORKING 3rd) (UNIT_TECH_REQ, state.py:54).
- purchase_pref pads `units` (10 each): SCOUT is always purchasable (:176-181 with rules.py:137-141) at 50 gold, so every gated turn above 120 gold buys a 5-strength scout — the weakest component per gold; MONUMENT/GRANARY (pop cap and income) sit behind it.
- Under the differential objective (value.py:62-71) wide play is doubly valuable: it raises own score and, via pop/science compounding, suppresses the max rival.

## Expected failure modes
- Settler pipeline is slow: at 5 prod/turn a pop-1 city needs ~16 turns per settler (80 cost, state.py:33; engine.py:30-33), so city #3 lands mid-teens and #4 around turn 28-32 — too late to compound within max_turns=40 (configs/llm-vs-turtler-005.yaml:7).
- Cap corruption: visible foreign cities count toward `max_cities` (scripted.py:81 + visibility.py:178-200), so mid-game border contact can silently switch settler production off at 2-3 own cities — precisely when the doctrine's advantage should accrue.
- Wasted budget by design: one step per unit per turn (:157-161) discards half of mv-2 units' movement; marchers never heal (:51-57 engine.py); every turn burns the completeness bounce and the fallback fortify that the next move undoes (rules.py:429-431).
- Hardcoded march targets (4,−3)/(−4,3) (:109) are duel-axis points (starts at layouts.py:26): on the radius-7 4-seat map, pids 1-3 all march to the same tile — contact timing is seat luck, not doctrine; units also arrive unfortified and wounded.
- Opportunistic-only combat: no approach-to-attack logic (only the blind march), no focus fire, so it trades 10-pt units piecemeal into fortified defenders (+4, rules.py:448) and eats melee counters (:452-459) — roughly a 20-strength attacker does ~24 dmg to an unfortified 20 and ~16 to a fortified spearman (rules.py:87-90).
- Gold management: the ≥120 gate with a 50-cost first pref means gold pools above the threshold and drains on scouts instead of granaries that lift the pop cap (engine.py:36-37).

## Variance sensitivity
- Seat position: high. March and blocked-settler targets branch on `pid == 0` only (:109, :153), so three of four seats share one destination on the 4-seat map; sequential phase order (simulator.py:91-96) gives earlier seats first claim on contested founding ground. The map layer was hard-pinned to 2 seats (now parameterized — Lane A) and the hardcoded geometry still needs the planned `_march_target` seam.
- Map seed: medium-high. Terrain is a per-tile weighted roll (layouts.py:28-35); ring-2+ food baskets set growth timing (engine.py:70-91); mountains/coast choke both the 1-step march and settler walks (settlers retry among 3 geometrically-nearest candidates with no terrain planning, :45-50); the corridor carve no longer maps cleanly onto 4-way symmetry. Combat outcomes are seed-locked but action-sequence dependent (rules.py:87-90).
- Opponent count (2→4): high variance, higher ceiling. Own yields don't depend on rivals, but the max-rival differential (value.py:62-71) punishes any seat that snowballs while you fight a third party; more neighbors means more visible foreign cities (cap corruption), more border contact for the attack-on-sight rule, and more contested spots inside the hardcoded push lanes. This is the doctrine the planned keys most directly parameterize: `expand_ring` generalizes the (2,2)/(−2,−2) settler fallback (:153) and push distance, `aggression` replaces the hardcoded 2-attack cap (:103) that currently fronts its unfortified marchers into unfavorable trades.
