# Threat-responsive growth foundations

Date: 2026-09-06. This isolated candidate supplies an opt-in pure `GrowthPolicy`, an optional `choose_production(..., growth_policy=...)` hook, and additive native production capability observations. It does not wire the policy into `StrategicController`, change the model directive, move a unit, found a city, or enable a live configuration. The existing production policy remains the default when no growth policy is supplied.

## Verified findings

The reviewed starting source at `76f4dce0a344f749d6ddbea42b4f92dcba9d649e` uses a five-ID opening defender list and defaults one owned-plus-queued copy of each other unit type. This can prefer multiple military types after individual targets are met. Settlement is only an ephemeral `found_city` override; routine unassigned settlers hold. Existing native `found_city` already asks `UnitManager.CanStartOperation` and issues the supported operation through the audited adapter. This candidate adds no native operation.

The new production hook classifies exact currently offered unit IDs using observed capabilities, including modern units; it does not expand the opening ID list. `available_production_read` adds optional Combat, RangedCombat, Domain, FoundCity and BuildCharges columns to unit rows. Buildings retain their existing four fields. The strict parser accepts historical four-field rows and the complete nine-field unit extension. Unknown values remain `None`; domains outside LAND/SEA/AIR, unavailable numeric fields and unavailable founding flags emit `?`, rather than fabricated capability. An incomplete/malformed extension or capability fields on a building fail. Exact installed schema/XML source paths and SHA-256 values are retained in `runs/threat-growth-evidence-20260906/native-source-proof.json`; they establish base source fields, not a fresh active-ruleset observation. The local translated-Lua fixture establishes the wrapper's behavior with supplied GameInfo rows, not native engine compatibility.

`GrowthPolicy.begin_turn(state, turn=..., catalogs=...)` accepts only bounded projected entities, terrain and owned-city catalogs. It binds one player and consecutive completed own turns. `complete_turn` must be called after successful controller closure, never after a failed turn. Repeated economic reads cannot advance the quiet-turn clock. No wall clocks, model calls, save restoration or simulator changes enter this state.

A currently observed `is_barbarian is True` contact within the configurable five-hex city radius enters defend immediately. A later economy refresh can also enter defend. Three consecutive clear own-turn observations are required to return to grow; the threshold is configurable from one to ten turns and is a heuristic. Other visible players are recorded as unclassified/nonbarbarian contacts; their presence does not establish war or hostile intent. Fog threats remain unknown. Routes avoid all visible foreign contacts conservatively without relabeling them enemies.

Risk audits compare observed local barbarian strength, unknown threat strengths, healthy local defense strength, the military count shortfall, queued/reserved strength and the resulting production need. Strength is the maximum of observed melee/ranged strength; summed strength and the 1.25 threat multiplier are planning heuristics, not combat odds. Healthy means `health_valid is True`, numeric hp/max_hp, positive max_hp and at least 90% observed health. Missing legacy health metadata, wounded units and distant troops do not count as healthy local defense. They remain inventory. This aligns with the separate recovery overlay's 90% resume threshold without altering or duplicating its recovery state.

The default land-military floor is one unit per city; a local threat can raise it to observed threat count plus one, subject to a default empire cap of eight. Healthy current strength is compared with a planning target of at least 20 per city and 1.25 times known local threat strength. Queued units and accepted unobserved reservations reduce future production demand, but do not become actual healthy defenders or escorts. All owned city queues count, including remembered exact capability types absent from the active city's offered catalog. Unknown queued capacity is explicitly reserved against the cap without claiming military strength. Positive observed strength or positive native combat capability identifies global combat inventory across land, sea and air; unavailable-strength/unclassified owned units reserve uncertain capacity. A present capability object with unknown combat fields also reserves uncertain queued capacity. Known naval/air queues consume the empire military cap but provide no land-defense count or projected land-defense strength. Land defense, city guards and escorts require explicit observed LAND combat capability in addition to current healthy-state checks. No unit is disbanded, upgraded or re-tasked by this policy.

When defense is needed, eligible land combat units compete using bounded observed strength per reported production turn, then explicit recurring preferences and exact IDs. This score caps marginal strength at the current planning gap (minimum 20) to limit overkill preference; unknown/nonpositive reported turns supply no efficiency score. Unknown capability, unsupported naval/air domains and unneeded civilian roles are not guessed from unit names. A satisfied minimum army releases production to a feasible escorted settler opportunity, then infrastructure/builders. Builder and settler totals aggregate compatible exact types across owned units, every queue and reservations. Explicit per-item `unit_targets`, including zero, remain upper bounds; an explicit SCOUT role exclusion still suppresses scout production. If nothing is eligible, the hook returns an explicit no-choice result; it does not exceed the limits to fill a queue.

A single fresh-only settlement mission retains mission ID, settler ID, site, escort ID and creation turn. A site must be currently owner-observed, unowned known land, free of observed city occupancy, conservatively spaced from known cities and away from visible foreign contacts. Only currently owner-observed land corridors are searched; remembered terrain lacking owner fields cannot establish a safe route. At most 64 otherwise eligible sites receive route probes, each visiting at most 2,048 cells and at most 12 steps by default. City spacing, normalized terrain and projected axial paths are planning filters, never proof of native founding legality, embarkation, map wrap or path costs. Known-neighbor coverage is an information score, not yield/resource/freshwater quality.

Each city first reserves distinct healthy nearby guards; a separate healthy spare within one hex of the prospective settler/origin is required as an escort. Queues and distant or recovering troops cannot satisfy this check. An owned settler's health and remaining movement must also be verified before a step proposal. The mission revalidates during each turn and production choice, so losing an escort, acquiring a city, new danger, lost ownership, unknown terrain or TTL expiration suppresses stale proposals. Tactical or recovery interruption does not overwrite the site or identity. An observed owned city at the site is recorded with `observed_owned_city_not_causal_receipt`, without claiming this policy founded it. Expired, lost-settler and city-observed terminal missions remain latched and inspectable. A later missing city or returning unit cannot restart the same terminal mission before its TTL. Explicit reviewed retirement/replanning remains required.
